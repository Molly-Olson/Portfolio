Our Story

Family Owned Since 2001

Linda and Dave Kowalski met as teenagers at a diner not unlike this one — Dave washing dishes, Linda waiting tables. They always said someday they'd have a place of their own. In May 2001, that someday finally arrived on Legion Way SE, just a short walk from the Washington State Capitol dome.

Over two decades later, the Kowalskis still open the doors themselves most mornings. Linda runs the kitchen, refining the same biscuit recipe she's been making since day one. Dave works the floor, knowing the regulars by name.
