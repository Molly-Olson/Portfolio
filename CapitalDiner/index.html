Our Story

Built on Early Mornings and Good Company

Linda and Dave Kowalski met as teenagers at a diner not unlike this one — Dave washing dishes, Linda waiting tables. They always said someday they'd have a place of their own. In May 2001, that someday finally arrived when they opened The Capital Diner in a little corner spot on Capitol Way South, just a short walk from the Washington State Capitol dome.

Over two decades later, the Kowalskis still open the doors themselves most mornings. Linda runs the kitchen, refining the same biscuit recipe she's been making since day one. Dave works the floor, knowing the regulars by name — and usually knowing their order before they sit down.

We don't do trendy. We do consistent. The same hash browns, the same sausage gravy, the same warm, unhurried atmosphere that Olympia's nurses, lawmakers, students, and families have relied on every morning for more than twenty years.

"Just good food, good coffee, and good people. That's all we ever wanted to be."

— Linda & Dave Kowalski, Owners
