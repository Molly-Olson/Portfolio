Why Choose Us

We Treat Your Yard
Like It's Our Own

There's no shortage of lawn crews out there. Here's what sets GreenThumb apart — and why our clients keep calling us back season after season.

Reliable & On-Time, Every Time

We show up when we say we will — no excuses, no rescheduling at the last minute. Your scheduled visits are locked in, and our crew is there rain or shine (unless weather makes the work unsafe or counterproductive).

Fully Insured & Licensed

GreenThumb carries full general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage on every job. You're never on the hook if something unexpected happens on your property. Licensed by the state — we don't cut corners, legally or otherwise.

Satisfaction Guaranteed

We don't leave until you're happy. If something isn't right, we make it right — same day, no extra charge. Our reputation is built on referrals, and we've earned every single one of them.

How It Works

From First Call to Finished Yard

1

Free Consultation

We come to you. Walk us around your property, tell us what's bugging you and what you're dreaming about. No pressure, no obligation — just honest conversation.

We put together a detailed plan and a clear, itemized quote. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons. You'll know exactly what we're doing and exactly what it costs.

4

Ongoing Care
