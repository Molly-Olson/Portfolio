About Us

Rooted in the
South Sound

GreenThumb Landscaping has been designing and maintaining exceptional outdoor spaces across Lacey, Olympia, Tumwater, and the greater South Sound region since 2009.

We started as a small crew with a single truck and a belief that landscaping done right should be indistinguishable from landscape architecture. Seventeen years later, that belief hasn't changed — only our capacity to deliver it has grown.

Every project is led by a senior designer and executed by a crew that cares about the details: the way a border is edged, how mulch settles against stone, the spacing that lets a planting breathe and fill in over three seasons. We're licensed, insured, and proud to be a local business that stays local.
