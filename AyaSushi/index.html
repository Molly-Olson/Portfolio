Our Story

Crafted with
passion & precision

Nestled in West Olympia, Aya Sushi has earned its reputation as the city's premier destination for authentic Japanese cuisine. Beautifully decorated and immaculately clean, every detail of the dining experience has been thoughtfully considered.

From the first bite of a signature roll to the last sip of miso soup, you'll taste the difference that comes from using only the freshest ingredients and a kitchen that genuinely cares about its craft.

Whether you're coming in for a quick lunch, a date night, or a family meal — Aya Sushi delivers every time.
