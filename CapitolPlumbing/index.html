Our Story

Three Generations of Trusted Plumbing

Capitol Plumbing & Drain was founded in 1984 by Harold Hendricks, a master plumber who believed that homeowners deserved honest, skilled service from someone who lived in the same community. His son Dave took over in 2002, and today Dave's son Mike Hendricks leads the company as a third-generation master plumber with 18 years in the trade.

Mike grew up on job sites, apprenticed under his father, and earned his master plumber license in 2008. He's seen every kind of plumbing problem this region throws at a house — from the old cast-iron drain systems in South Capitol Hill bungalows to the new construction in Lacey's growing neighborhoods.

"My grandfather started this business on a handshake. That hasn't changed. We still show up on time, do the work right, and make sure you're happy before we leave."

— Mike Hendricks, Owner & Master Plumber
