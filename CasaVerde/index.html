Our Story

A Family Table, Open to Everyone

Casa Verde opened its doors in 2009 with a simple promise: cook the food we grew up eating and serve it with the same love our grandmother showed every guest at her table. What began as a twelve-seat family operation has grown into a beloved neighborhood institution — but the heart of it has never changed.

Founders Elena and Miguel Ramirez brought their recipes from the valleys of Oaxaca, where food isn't just sustenance — it's ceremony. Their mole negro, still made from scratch every Tuesday, takes four hours and calls for twenty-three ingredients. Their tamales are hand-tied every holiday season, just as Elena's mother taught her.

Today, their daughter Sofia runs the kitchen, carrying those traditions forward while bringing her own culinary training to the menu. We source from three local farms within thirty miles, we grind our own chiles, and we make every tortilla by hand. That's not a marketing line — it's just how we do things.

"When you eat at Casa Verde, you're sitting at our family table. We want you to leave full, happy, and already planning when you'll come back." — Elena Ramirez, Co-Founder
