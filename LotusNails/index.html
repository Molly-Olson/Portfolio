Our Story

More Than a Nail Salon

Lotus Nail Studio was founded by May Nguyen in 2015 with one mission: to create a clean, welcoming space where every client feels like royalty. What started as a modest studio on Olympia's Eastside has grown into one of the most trusted names in nail care in Thurston County — built entirely on word of mouth, five-star reviews, and genuine relationships with our clients.

We believe that a great nail appointment is more than polish and shine. It's an hour of quiet, a moment of care, a small act of self-respect. That's why every station is sterilized between every client, every product is premium, and every technician is licensed, trained, and genuinely passionate about their craft.

Whether you're celebrating a milestone, decompressing after a hard week, or just treating yourself because you deserve it — Lotus is your place. Come as you are. Leave feeling beautiful.
