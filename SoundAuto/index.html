Our Story

Built on Trust, One Repair at a Time

Sound Auto Repair was founded in 1987 by Jim and Carol Hendricks. What started as a one-bay shop on Harrison Ave has grown into Olympia's most trusted independent repair shop — now serving over 12,000 customers across Thurston County.

Jim's son, Dave Hendricks, took over the day-to-day operations in 2009 and brought the same values his parents instilled from day one: fix it right, charge fairly, and treat every customer like a neighbor — because in Olympia, they probably are.

We're not a chain. We don't have sales quotas. When one of our techs looks at your car, they're telling you what it actually needs — not what will pad a commission. That philosophy has kept our customers coming back for 35 years, and it's not changing.
