Our Story

More Than Just a Haircut

Founded in 2018, Fresh Cuts has been the neighborhood's go-to spot for clean, precise cuts and a genuine barbershop experience. What started as a single chair in a small studio has grown into a trusted destination for men of all ages — from the Friday night date-night fresh-up to the weekly maintenance crew who wouldn't go anywhere else.

Owner and master barber Marcus Webb built Fresh Cuts on a simple belief: every person who sits in the chair deserves undivided attention, skill, and a cut they're proud to walk out with. That philosophy drives everything we do — from the products we use to the music we play.

Licensed & Experienced

All our barbers are fully licensed with a combined 20+ years of experience.

Premium Products Only

We use top-shelf grooming products — because your hair deserves the best.

Community First
