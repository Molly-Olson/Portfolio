Our Story
"Every person who sits in the chair deserves undivided attention, skill, and a cut they're proud to walk out with."
— Marcus Webb, Owner & Master Barber

Founded in 2018, Fresh Cuts has been Olympia's go-to spot for clean, precise cuts and a genuine barbershop experience. What started as a single chair in a small studio has grown into a trusted neighborhood destination for men of all ages.

Licensed & Experienced All barbers fully licensed — 20+ combined years of skill.
Premium Products Only Top-shelf grooming products, because your hair deserves the best.
Community First Proud to serve this neighborhood and give back whenever we can.
