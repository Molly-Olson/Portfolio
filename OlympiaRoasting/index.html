Our Story

Built on Coffee, Community & Craft

When Sam and Priya Chen moved to Olympia from Portland in 2017, they had one shared obsession: finding a coffee shop that took the bean as seriously as the experience around it. They couldn't find one.

So in March 2018, they opened Olympia Roasting Co. in a converted 1920s storefront on Capitol Way. What started as a 12-seat café with a used Probat roaster and a hand-painted sign has grown into a beloved neighborhood anchor — without losing a single thing that made it special.

Sam handles the sourcing, traveling twice a year to origin countries to meet the farmers whose work ends up in your cup. Priya runs operations and is responsible for nearly every recipe on the menu — including those cardamom morning buns that sell out by 9am on weekends.

Relationships First We pay above Fair Trade minimums to every farmer we work with.
Low Waste Operation Spent grounds go to local community gardens. Cups are compostable.
Community Space Free WiFi, no time limits, real talk. Stay as long as you want.
